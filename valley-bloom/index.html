How we build it together

One

The design session

We get on a call and play. Who is this couple, what does she care about, what has she already saved. Colour, type, the feel of the first screen, which photographs carry the brand. We go through your portfolio together and pull the images that do the work.

You know flowers better than anyone I could hire. I know how this needs to behave online. The session is where those two things meet.

Two

I build the first draft

The store, the collection pages, the palettes, the copy, the questions couples always ask answered before they ask them. The booking calendar with your blackout dates and your order limits, so the store can only ever sell a date you can actually cover.

You do nothing during this stage except send photos when I ask.

Three

We refine it

You walk the whole thing and tell me what is wrong. Two full rounds are included, and in practice I keep going until it feels like yours. This is where a store stops looking like a template and starts looking like a florist made it.

Four

Launch, then four months covered

We go live. For four months I look after it. Broken things, small changes, a new collection, a price adjustment, a photo swap, a question about how something works. You are not paying a separate bill while you are still finding your feet.

What stays yours

I would rather tell you this now than surprise you later.

  • The Shopify account opens in your name, on your card. I work inside it as a collaborator. You own the store outright and you can remove me at any time.
  • Shopify Basic is forty nine Canadian a month, or thirty seven a month if you pay for the year up front. Basic covers everything this store needs. With the booking app added, expect seventy to a hundred and ten Canadian a month to run.
  • The Shopify plan starts when the build starts, not at launch, because the theme and the apps need a live account to sit on. That is roughly one month of the plan before anything is earning. I would rather you see that now than on a statement.
  • The theme is a one time purchase, roughly three to five hundred Canadian, licensed to your store and yours to keep.
  • One photography session. Your editorial work sells the brand, but each collection needs one honest photograph of exactly what arrives. Showing an arch on a nine hundred dollar page creates the disappointment this whole model exists to avoid.
  • The name and the logo. I do not do branding strategy and I am not going to pretend otherwise. Bring me a name and a mark, or a designer you like, and I will build everything around it.
