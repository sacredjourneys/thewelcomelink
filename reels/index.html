How it works

From your photos to a finished reel in about five days.

  1. 01

    Send your photos

    Your best 6 to 10 listing photos. Whatever is already on Airbnb or your site works.

  2. 02

    I build the shots

    I pick the strongest angles and animate each one with subtle, believable motion.

  3. 03

    Every frame gets checked

    Anything that drifts from your real space, a chair that changes, a wave that isn't there, gets cut.

  4. 04

    Ready to post

    You get a vertical reel and a wide version, with text and your name on the end card.
