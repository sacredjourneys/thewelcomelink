The coast

Your nearest beaches

🏄
La Punta 600m away. The lively one — surfers, scene, and the best sunsets in Puerto. Walk down or take a scooter. 📍 Map
🌿
Playa La Barra Same distance, completely different feel. Secluded, natural, almost no one there — perfect for a quiet sunset. The road down is rough at times; fine on foot, bike, or scooter. 📍 Map

Birria y barbacoa Alvarado · local spot · Fri–Sun 8 AM–3 PM

Weekends only. No website, no real online presence — until recently they weren't even on Maps. The kind of place you only knew about if someone local told you. We've been everywhere. This is the best quesabirria in Puerto.
