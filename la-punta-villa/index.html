Before you come

A little prep

A few easy things worth sorting in the days before you travel.

💵
Bring some cashMany of the taco stands, beach spots, and small cafés in La Punta are cash-only, and small bills help. There are ATMs in town, though they sometimes run empty on weekends, so take out a little early.
🚕
Sort your airport pickupPuerto Escondido airport (PXM) is about ten minutes away. Message me before you fly in and I'll arrange a trusted driver, so someone is waiting for you when you land.
🧴
Pack for sand and sunReef-safe sunscreen, a hat, and flip-flops. There's an outdoor shower to rinse off after the beach. One honest guest tip: bring your own shampoo.
📅
Pre-book any experiencesSurf lessons, a boat tour to the dolphins or the glowing lagoon, a massage here at the villa. A few need a little notice. Tell me your dates and I'll have it arranged before you arrive.
Getting here

Finding your way to the villa

You're right in the heart of La Punta, a short hop from the airport. Tap your route below. Every one ends at the garden gate, where I'll meet you and get you settled.

From the airport (PXM)Taxi · 10 min›
  1. Puerto Escondido airport (PXM) is about a ten-minute drive from the villa.
  2. Take an official airport taxi, or the driver I arrange for you. Show them the map pin below, or ask for La Punta, near Playa Zicatela's La Punta entrance.
  3. Message me when you land and I'll be ready to meet you at the gate.
  4. A street taxi runs roughly $150 to $250 pesos. The driver I set up will have a fixed price we agree beforehand.
From Oaxaca CityVan / bus›
  1. From Oaxaca City, take an overnight ADO bus or a shared van down to Puerto Escondido (about 6 to 7 hours on the mountain road).
  2. From the Puerto Escondido terminal, it's a ten-minute taxi to La Punta.
  3. Ask the driver for La Punta and show them the map pin below.
  4. Message me when you're close and I'll meet you at the gate.
By carFree parking›
  1. Head for La Punta in Brisas de Zicatela and follow the map pin below to the villa.
  2. There's free street parking right out front. Guests almost always find a spot by the door.
  3. Message me when you arrive and I'll come let you in.
Already in La Punta · on foot2 min walk›
  1. From the La Punta beach entrance, the villa is about a two-minute walk, roughly 100 meters.
  2. Follow the map pin to the garden's one secured entrance, shared by just two villas.
  3. Message me when you're at the gate and I'll let you in.

While you're here

Experiences in La Punta

These aren't tourist add-ons, they're the real life of this place, with people who actually live and work here. Every experience is bookable on request, just message me. Prices are shared privately and can shift with the season, but almost anything is possible.

Tell me the kind of trip you're after, surf and sunsets, or slow and quiet, and I'll point you to the right people.

🏄

Surf lessons

La Punta is one of the best places in the world to learn. I'll connect you with local instructors who grew up on these waves. Board and rash guard included.

Book on request
🧘

Beach yoga

Open-air morning yoga a few steps away, facing the sea. A calm, grounding way to start the day. Most mornings, drop-in.

🐬

Dolphin & turtle boat trip

An early boat out from the coast to see dolphins and sea turtles, and whales in season. Calm water, soft light, a beautiful morning.

Book on request · seasonal
✨

Bioluminescence night tour

After dark, a boat into Laguna de Manialtepec, where the water glows blue when you move through it. Best on a moonless night.

Book on request · seasonal
🌅

Sunset at the point

Walk to the tip of La Punta for the evening sunset ritual. Everyone gathers on the rocks as the sky turns. No arranging needed, just go.

💆

Massage at the villa

A local therapist comes to you, here on the terrace or in the garden. Deep, restorative bodywork without leaving home.

Book on request
🍶

Mezcal & food tasting

A guided taste of Oaxacan mezcal and local food with someone who knows the makers and the stories behind them.

Book on request
🐢

Baby turtle release

In season, join an evening release of baby turtles on a nearby beach. Gentle and moving, lovely with kids.
