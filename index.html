The idea

It's not a guidebook. It's a welcome.

When a guest arrives and feels genuinely welcomed into a place someone loves, they don't just have a good stay. They fall in love with the town.

The rental is the doorway. What we're really giving them is the best possible experience of the place.

A guide that tells your guest where to get the best coffee, which beach to catch at sunset, which taqueria only locals know about. Happy guests, better reviews, more income. Those are the byproducts of doing one thing well: welcoming people into a place we love.

Why it works

The return

More than a welcome.

Guests reading a guide like this are already in planning mode, so they tend to book the experiences and add-ons you suggest, a surf lesson, a stocked fridge, a late checkout.
