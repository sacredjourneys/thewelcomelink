Questions

What hosts ask.

How much does a website cost?

A landing page starts at $350 USD ($6,650 MXN). A full villa site is $700 USD ($13,300 MXN). A collection site for several homes starts at $1,400 USD ($26,600 MXN). Every project is quoted to fit, and a welcome guide for each home is included.

Do I have to leave Airbnb?

No. Keep your listings. Your website gives returning guests, referrals and people who find you on Instagram a direct way to reach you and book, with no platform fees in between.

How do guests book?

They tap to message you on WhatsApp from the page of the home they like. You confirm the dates and payment directly with them.

Is the site in English and Spanish?

Yes. Every site is bilingual, with a switch at the top, so the same site works for guests from Mexico and from abroad.

Can it use my own domain?

Yes. We connect it to your own address, like tierraenarmonia.com.mx, so the site and the brand are fully yours.

Who keeps it updated?

The care plan takes care of it. For a small monthly fee we handle hosting, edits, new homes and keeping every guide current.

Send us your listing. We will tell you what we would build and what it would cost.
